Delete Your Account

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseDelete Account

Option 1: In the app

  • Open Pecky and go to Profile › Delete account.
  • Confirm. Your account and cloud data are deleted.

Option 2: Without the app

  • Email kartalkarabulutbusiness@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Apple or Google account.
  • Use the subject "Delete my Pecky account".
  • We delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (user ID, sign-in details, email and name shared by Apple or Google).
  • Your cloud backup: question photos, notes, answers, subjects, tags, reminders, review history, exam type and date.
  • Purchase and free-allowance records linked to your account.

What is kept

  • Content stored only on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data is deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
  • Payment records held by Apple or Google follow their own policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
